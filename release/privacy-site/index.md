# Privacy Policy — Marklet (Markdown Viewer)

**Effective date:** 2026-XX-XX
**App:** Marklet: Markdown Viewer (`com.marklet.md`)
**Developer:** Alrimi Studio
**Contact:** bellx3@hanmail.net

## 1. Summary

Marklet does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server.
There is no account, no login, no analytics, and no advertising.
Everything the app does happens on your device.

## 2. Your documents

- The app can open Markdown (`.md`) and text files that **you** choose, either by
  picking them inside the app or by opening a file from another app.
- File contents are read, rendered, and saved **entirely on your device**.
- **Document contents are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with the developer
  or with any third party.**
- When you save, the app writes back to the file you opened. It does not create
  copies on any server.

## 3. What is stored on your device

To provide a recent-documents list and to remember your reading preferences, the app
stores the following **locally, on your device only**:

- File names and the system file references (URIs) of documents you have opened
- Reading preferences (theme, font size, line-break option, and similar settings)
- A record of whether a tip purchase has been completed, so the supporter badge can
  be shown
- An automatically saved draft of a document while you are editing it, so that your
  work is not lost if the app is closed

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## 4. Permissions

- The app declares **no storage permissions**. It uses the Android Storage Access
  Framework, which means access is granted only for the specific files you select.
- The app declares the `INTERNET` permission for exactly two purposes:
  1. Processing tip purchases through Google Play's billing system.
  2. Opening a link in your browser when you tap a link inside a document.
  Document contents are not sent over the network for any reason.
- The app declares the `com.android.vending.BILLING` permission for optional tips.
- The app does **not** use the Android Advertising ID.

## 5. Remote images

Documents may reference images hosted on the internet. Loading such an image would
reveal your IP address to that server, so **this is turned off by default**. Remote
images are shown only after you turn the option on in Settings, or tap the individual
placeholder in the document.

## 6. Purchases

Tips are optional and the app is fully usable without them.
Purchases are processed by **Google Play's billing system**. The developer never
receives or has access to your payment card, bank, or Google account details.
The app receives only the product identifier and a transaction identifier, and stores
them on your device to avoid granting the same thank-you twice.

## 7. Children

This app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any
information from children.

## 8. Data deletion

Because no data is collected or stored off your device, there is nothing to request
deletion of. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data, including the
recent-documents list, drafts, and your preferences. Your own document files are not
deleted by uninstalling the app.
